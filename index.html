About

I research efficient inference for large language models — making models run where they don't naturally fit. My dissertation (advisor: Dr. Kun Suo, defense expected 2027) studies the regime where model weights exceed device memory: enforcing and predicting activation sparsity, and designing storage-aware inference systems for memory-constrained platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson.

My work is supported by U.S. National Science Foundation grants, and my first-author study of LLM deployment on edge devices has been cited by researchers at Meta, Google, NVIDIA, Stanford, MIT, and UC San Diego. Every paper ships verified, reproducible artifacts. Before the PhD, I built system-level Android software (AOSP/HAL) for the PockeTalk translation device at BJIT.
