About

I research efficient inference for large language models — making models run where they don't naturally fit. My dissertation (advisor: Dr. Kun Suo, defense expected 2027) studies the regime where model weights exceed device memory, spanning activation sparsity, activation-pattern prediction, and storage-aware system design for memory-constrained platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson.

My work is supported by U.S. National Science Foundation grants, and my first-author study of LLM deployment on edge devices has been cited by researchers at Meta, Google, NVIDIA, Stanford, MIT, and UC San Diego. I release verified, reproducible artifacts for my papers. Before the PhD, I built system-level Android software (AOSP/HAL) for the PockeTalk translation device at BJIT.
